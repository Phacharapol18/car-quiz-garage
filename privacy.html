Privacy Policy

Car Quiz Garage · Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

Car Quiz Garage does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or trackers.

Purchases

  • Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Your payment details are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. The app never sees them.
  • To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play on your device whether your Google account owns it, and remembers the answer on the device. Nothing is sent to us.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings, scores, leaderboard names, exam results, and question history are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage.
  • This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with "Clear" on the leaderboard, "Reset my progress" in the ASE Prep Garage, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for short haptic feedback on answers. It can be turned off in your device settings.
  • Google Play billing: used only to buy or restore Pro.
  • The app needs no internet connection to play. Buying or restoring Pro needs a connection to Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: open an issue at github.com/Phacharapol18/car-quiz-garage, or use the support email on the Google Play listing.
