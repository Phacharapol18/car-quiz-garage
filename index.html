    How to race

    • Right answer: 100 pts, plus up to 100 more for speed.
    • Nitro: 3 right in a row multiplies your points by ×1.5. Five in a row makes it ×2.
    • Pace car: it keeps moving while the clock runs. Have more points than its target when you cross the line.
    • Pit crew: one 50/50 and one +7 second fuel stop for each race.
    • Keys: 1–4 to answer, F for 50/50, T for more time, Enter for next, M to mute.
